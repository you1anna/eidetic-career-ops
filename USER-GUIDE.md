# Use work-ops

[First useful result](#your-first-useful-result) · [Jobs](#track-jobs-and-applications) ·
[People](#act-outside-the-site-then-record) · [Recovery](#when-something-goes-wrong)

Find worthwhile work, resume an existing process, and retrieve relationship context. Prepare wording
in **People → Send messages** or your usual communication tool, then send externally. The site keeps evidence and optional factual progress; it does not
require an email to be generated, approved, pasted or saved here.

In this demo snapshot the guide describes the private deployment. To try the screens, run the
workspace locally on synthetic data, as the README explains.

## Local outreach preview

Use the synthetic preview described in [local setup](ops/LOCAL.md), with invented records. Existing
saved previews and their wording are historical evidence, not instructions to resume their workflow.
In the current preview, try the four tasks under [validation](#validate-the-outreach-features).
No live data, external send or deployment is needed.

## Sign in

This guide follows the repository version; release status
identifies changes that have not reached the site yet.

Open [work.example.com](https://work.example.com) on your Mac or phone. Enter your authorized email address and press **Send login code**, then enter the six-digit code from the email. Sessions stay signed in for **one week** on each browser or device.

If a code does not arrive within a minute, check the newest message in your inbox or spam folder before requesting another code (requesting a new code invalidates the previous one).

## Your first useful result

1. Open **This week → Best opportunities**. Start with the first three cards: read the role,
   employer, pay/location, dated source, **Why this is near the top** and **Still unknown**. Expand
   **Evidence and comparisons**, or select the role name to open its full brief.
   Where a sourced duty is available, the remit paragraph quotes it and links to its source;
   requirements are not presented as duties. The full brief retains the original summary.
2. Use **Show next …** to reveal up to ten, or **See all … in Jobs → Discovery** for the complete
   collection. Compare suitability first; a stronger permanent role can rank above a weaker
   contract. Contract/interim preference breaks equal scores. Unknown engagement stays unknown.
3. Use a card's **Shortlist** when you want to pursue a job, or **Skip** to set it aside. Only valid
   actions are offered. Find the same record and history in **Jobs → Shortlisted** or **Closed /
   skipped**; **Undo** reverses a mistaken change if no later change would be overwritten.
4. Resume work under **Act now**, or expand **In progress** and **Waiting**. **Jobs → Active** also
   keeps it available. Use its recorded next step and progress controls. Opening an advert does
   not record an application.

An **Act now** job keeps its recorded next step. Where the only step is the default application
instruction, the compact card uses that space for the available fit evidence. It combines stated
terms and shows the remit and advert status; missing facts stay visible. Open the role name for
the complete decision check before choosing whether to apply.

When an advert is confirmed closed, a shortlist or unanswered application leaves **This week**
and routine research. Find it under **Jobs → History and reference → Closed / skipped**; its
application status and history remain intact. A recorded reply, interview, offer or explicit
next step can still keep the hiring process in your weekly work after the public advert closes.

You should finish with a decision about worthwhile work and a recoverable next action, without
administering email wording on the site.

## Your weekly routine

### Find a lead from a notification

Open **Dashboard → Site functions → Job alert email processing → Review unassessed email leads**.
The list shows up to 20 recent eligible email leads that have not yet been saved to Jobs, newest
first. Use **Search email leads**, optionally select **Email source**, then **Search** to find an
older company or title. Repeated sightings of the same source advert appear once. This is an
optional way to recover a lead you already noticed, not a backlog you need to clear.

Select **Review** to open the matching lead. **Unassessed** means the email is not a researched
recommendation. **Email says — unverified** keeps the original source, stated pay/location and
expandable **Original email summary** together. **Open source advert** opens the source;
**Find original email in Gmail** searches its Message-ID in your signed-in mailbox, while
**Download original email** saves the retained `.eml`. If an original is unavailable, the page
says so. The **Review link** opens this same lead directly and can be copied like any other link.
ChatGPT notifications are separate: this feature does not modify their wording or add buttons to them.

Choose **Shortlist** only when you want to pursue the role. It bypasses the automatic addition
limit and places the job in **This week → Act now** and **Jobs → Shortlisted**. It does not confirm
fit or vacancy availability, copy the email summary/pay into the saved role, or record an application.
Later alerts can update source-stated pay/location through the normal import rules; those terms
help filtering but do not establish fit or confirm that the vacancy is open.
The job offers its advert and suggested CV; **Review email context** returns to the original lead.
After you actually submit an application, use **I applied**. An existing application keeps its status.

If saving reports that the shortlist was saved but refresh failed, reload before trying again;
the same source URL cannot create a second role. If a lead is absent, search Jobs too: saved
roles leave this list. Missing/corrupt pending evidence shows an error and **Search** retries it;
a failed direct link offers **Retry**. A source that is closed or no longer eligible offers no
shortcut to shortlist it. Your existing records and original mail remain available.

### Review your current work

- Start with **Act now** for recorded actions and actionable processes. **Waiting** holds recorded
  waits; **In progress** keeps active work whose responsibility is unclear. A free-text next step
  does not establish who owes the next action. For a job, open its record and use **Update work state**
  to maintain **Next action**; an incoming hiring process keeps its recorded process next step.
  People and direct-work approaches offer **Update next step**. Record only events that happened.
- Review **Best opportunities**, initially three and expandable to ten. Supported remit and
  requirements dominate the score; titles, recency, contacts and practical terms have bounded
  influence. Missing evidence for an essential requirement means **not established**, rather than
  an assertion that you lack it, and prevents a strong recommendation. The visible reason comes
  from the score or selection constraint; **Evidence and comparisons** retains the detail.
- **Needs checking** is folded by default. Open it to review up to six research candidates, their
  evidence gaps and actual last attempts. Use **Shortlist** for roles you want to keep and **Skip**
  for unsuitable ones; reviewing them is optional. The regular research passes are at **09:00 and
  19:00 London time**, subject to limits and service availability; a particular role is not promised
  a check at the next pass. No recorded attempt means none is known. Opening the page starts no
  research, and an attempt date does not itself confirm that an advert is open.
- A promising Reed or Adzuna advert is not shown in Needs checking: its listing decides
  availability and research does not read those pages. It stays in **History and reference → Reference**; **Shortlist**
  it to have research check it. `wo approach` and the record's selection data name this rule.
- An advert enters **Best opportunities** only while it still looks open: its source listed it in
  the daily read, or research confirmed it, within the last 48 hours. A company or recorded lead
  enters only with a current, specific sourced need; a promising lead without one goes to Needs
  checking instead. **See all … in Jobs → Discovery** includes recommended adverts no longer
  confirmed open; nothing is deleted.
- Funding, a company's product description and hiring activity alone remain context. A vacancy
  supports consideration of that advertised role, not a second recommendation to sell the company
  consulting services. Direct-work recommendations need a sourced request or current delivery remit.
- Use **Jobs** for **Discovery**, **Shortlisted** and **Active**. **History and reference** holds
  **Closed / skipped** and **Reference**, without a backlog count.
  A previous skip or exclusion is preserved. Reference records remain searchable without being recommended or requiring review.
- Open **People** when you choose to contact someone. Personal outreach stays there, without a
  weekly quota, automatic personal follow-up or time-based no-response status.

A company logo, where available, appears beside its name. The browser requests it only when the
image enters view, sends no referrer and keeps a fixed space. Initials remain until loading succeeds
and return if loading fails; an agency's logo does not establish the identity of its client. Missing
images do not hide the role, evidence or controls.

### Choose your people

Search **People** by name and open the person. **Needs attention** puts replies and recorded next
steps first, then waiting contacts by age, people connected to **Act now** roles and your saved
priorities. Each person appears once. The People navigation count is the unique people in this queue;
agency desks are separate. **First priority** and **Next** remain your saved preferences, not promises
to contact someone today. They do not select This week or enrol people in scheduled research.

Choose **For Act now**, **Needs reply** or **Waiting** to filter the people shown. Action chips also
filter. Linked role titles open the role in **Jobs**; the back control returns to the person. A recorded
employer, agency or historical connection is a possible route, not proof of hiring authority. Open
the person for **Do this next** and contact controls; relationship and source context is folded below.

**Contact** opens your email app, LinkedIn or the recorded phone channel. It uses the selected or
preferred channel where available, otherwise recorded email, LinkedIn or phone details. Opening
the channel sends nothing and does not log contact. If no usable route is recorded, open the
person's details to add one. Use **Log contact** only after the conversation or message happened.

Waiting contacts show elapsed calendar days from the recorded contact date. Missing or future dates
do not acquire a guessed age. An agreed date is preserved as recorded; this queue does not treat it
as a deadline. Elapsed time alone never makes a person overdue or changes their next step.

If someone is not appropriate or not someone you want to speak to, open **More** on their row or sheet,
then choose **Demote** or **Demote to Not contacting**. That clears any priority and moves them to the folded
**Not contacting** list at the bottom of **People**; **Undo** reverses a mistaken tap. While in **Not
contacting**, they are excluded from contact routes, agency desks, outreach and research until you
press **Promote** (or **Promote to current contacts** on their sheet) to return them to **Current
contacts**.

Choose **Recruiters & agencies** to narrow People to saved recruiters and people linked to a recruitment
agency, **Current contacts** for your priority and known contacts, or **Not contacting** to review
demoted entries. **Agencies to approach** initially shows up to five agencies with relevant roles
currently confirmed open. Each role shows its availability and check date. An old advert, a warm
consultant or researched desk alone does not qualify an agency for this list. Open the agency for
the source evidence and consultant details; a linked consultant is not necessarily the person
handling each vacancy. Unconfirmed roles and older desks remain searchable. Choose **Everyone**
to return to all your people.

## Prepare the approach

Use the evidence and your own judgement to prepare in Gmail or your usual communication tool.
**Contact** can open an empty email composer; write and send the message there.

### Check the context

For work, read the single **Work assessment**. A company becomes a discovery lead only with a
specific sourced request or current delivery remit; creating an opportunity record alone does not
establish demand. Funding, a senior job title or a
contract award alone does not establish demand for your services. Source checks, fit and commercial
progress are separate facts. Generated wording is never independent evidence.

Engagement terms use the same assessment on the card and in the brief. **Engagement unresolved ·
source terms conflict** means the retained sources disagree; inspect the evidence before relying on
either label. **Engagement not stated** does not establish contract or permanent work.

For a person, read the relationship context and dated contact evidence. A public profile does not
verify a different email address or establish hiring authority. Unknown facts remain unknown.

### Weekly preparation

Weekly message preparation is retired. Scheduled research now maintains evidence for active and
shortlisted work plus the first ten discovery opportunities. See [research status](#job-and-contact-research).

### The note

Existing saved versions and sent wording remain read-only in **Sources and details → History**,
including replaced versions. Prepare new messages through [Send messages](#send-a-batch-of-messages).
Gmail edits and replies are not automatically synced to the site. For previous cover letters, CVs
and wording outside site history, use the [private reference-data map](docs/reference-data.md).

### Templates

The **Template** picker in **People → Send messages** uses the canonical repository template pack,
including its follow-up wording. It does not read your Gmail drafts or the complete message-library
archive. **Details & template notes** retains the pack's factual questions for you to check before
reusing older wording. Old template-page links still lead to Jobs.

### Send a batch of messages

1. In **People**, choose **Send messages**. Search by name or company and tick up to ten people,
   then choose **Start batch**. Only people with a currently available contact action appear.
   Search shows at most twenty matches; narrow your search to find someone else.
2. Choose **Channel**, then **Template → Use template**, or type directly in **Message to personalise**.
   Names and recorded context fill what is known. Replace the remaining `{{placeholders}}` before
   copying or opening the channel. Switching templates asks before replacing your text; changing
   channel preserves your edits. Use the template again if you want its channel-specific version.
3. Choose **Copy message** and open the channel. Gmail and WhatsApp prefill a short-enough message
   where a usable route is recorded; longer messages use copy/paste. LinkedIn opens the profile.
   Instagram opens your inbox, where you choose the person's conversation. **Other** supports manual
   sending in another app. Check the recipient, attachments and wording there, then send yourself.
4. Return and choose **Sent & next**. This records contact now, keeps the current next step and
   advances to the next person. It does not store the prepared text as evidence of what was sent.
   Instagram is recorded as **Other**, with **Instagram** in the contact note. To record a message
   already sent, choose its channel and press **Sent & next** without preparing wording.
5. **Previous** and **Skip for now** keep each person's text for this open batch and record nothing.
   **Finish batch** skips the last person without recording a send. **Undo send to …** corrects the
   most recent send recorded in the batch, retaining its history.

If a save fails, the person and wording stay on screen. Retry a connection failure. For a stale
record, choose **Refresh contact after a conflict**, review the refreshed context, then try again.
If the contact is no longer eligible, skip them. **Retry templates** recovers a failed template load;
you can also type your own message. If copying fails, the text is selected for your device's Copy.

Prepared text lasts only while the batch stays open: copy anything you want to keep before closing
or reloading. Successful contact records survive reload. Replies are recorded through the existing
person controls. Opening an app never records a send or schedules a follow-up. Local Chromium and
iPhone WebKit tests cover this flow; physical-device and deployed usability are separate checks.

### Talking points & CVs

In a role, choose **CV for this job**, then **Save CV choice**. The chooser and save button appear
above **Open CV ↗**, alongside Ready/Draft and the reason for the title-based suggestion. Your saved
choice is separate from that suggestion and survives reopening the role. **Use suggestion** restores
the current automatic choice when saved; it can change if the role's title changes. Check it against
the actual work: a title match is not proof of fit, and a Draft CV still needs your review.

**Open CV ↗** opens the saved choice's Google Doc. After changing the chooser, save before opening
it. Neither choosing a CV nor opening its link records an application. If a save says the record
changed elsewhere, your selection remains on screen. Note it, close the role (confirm discarding
the unsaved choice), select **Refresh**, reopen the role and review the current choice before retrying.

### Approve exact wording

Historical draft decisions remain read-only with their existing answers and history. Retirement
does not approve, withdraw or resolve them. Commercial and operational decisions retain their
normal controls.

## Act outside the site, then record

Compose, edit and send in Gmail or your usual communication tool. Tracking is optional. In **People**,
**Log contact** records the channel, an optional factual **Contact note** and **What happens next?**.
It never attaches a saved draft or asks for the sent message. The form records contact today;
it does not import a mailbox or infer the original send time.

**What happens next?** can retain the current step, record your action or what you are waiting for,
complete it, or explicitly clear it. A date is only for something actually agreed. **Update next step**
changes that commitment without recording another contact. **They replied**, **We spoke** and the
other offered progress controls record the event you report. Enter its relevant note before saving.

A contact's **Undo** restores its earlier step only if no later step edit would be overwritten.
Reloading preserves successful saves; failed saves keep the input. Personal records stay in People.
**Hand over to Work** is for a concrete opportunity and lets you review the carried next step.

### Referral & recruiter overlap warning

**CV submitted** requires the destination company. If a submission and a referral target the same
company, **Check first** helps you coordinate. It is an advisory based on recorded facts, not a
pre-send guarantee.

## Validate the outreach features

Check release status first. Use a local synthetic preview for
unreleased controls; once released, open [the live site](https://work.example.com/#week)
and reload. Try all four tasks on your Mac, then repeat the key actions on your iPhone.
Use real intentions and events; you do not need to send an email or invent contact history.

1. **Explain and choose worthwhile work.** In **This week → Best opportunities**, explain the
   initial recommendations' work, source, reason, uncertainty and next action within **60 seconds**.
   Repeat with a missing or failed logo. Expand **Evidence and comparisons** to check the support.
   Follow **Show next …** or **See all … in Jobs → Discovery** as needed. If you want to pursue a
   job, use its **Shortlist**, then find the same role in **Jobs → Shortlisted**.
   Use **Undo** if you want to reverse that shortlist. Pass: useful opportunities are easy to find,
   evidence supports the decision, and personal outreach tasks are absent from This week.
2. **Compare a permanent role.** Open **Jobs → Discovery** and find a permanent role, revealing
   more entries if needed. Open its brief and compare remit, fit, pay/location and unknowns with
   a contract where available. Pass: the engagement is correctly labelled and you can explain the tradeoff;
   a permanent advert is not presented as separate consulting demand. If none is available,
   report that as a discovery gap rather than treating the task as passed.
3. **Resume active work.** Open a real process from **Act now**, **In progress**, **Waiting** or
   **Jobs → Active**. Check that an explicit wait is labelled accurately and unclear responsibility
   remains **In progress**.
   Confirm its stage and history. For a job, open **Sources and details → Update work state** and
   set **Next action** to your actual next action; for an incoming process use its hiring-process
   controls. Save, reload, reopen it, and check the value persisted and the stage is still correct.
   Pass: you can resume the process without reconstructing it or pasting email text. Correct any
   mistaken entry through its existing controls; do not record an application or reply just to test.
4. **Retrieve relationship context.** In **People**, search for someone you know and open them.
   Find the relationship context, previous interactions and **History**. If they have saved drafts,
   expand **Saved wording** and confirm it remains readable. If you have an actual next step to
   update, use **Update next step**, save and reopen it. Pass: the context is useful without visiting
   This week, generating an email or logging contact merely to access the information. Compose
   and send in Gmail when you choose.

For each task report **pass / friction / blocked**, the record name, the step where you got stuck,
and what you expected. Note missing or poor opportunities separately from a broken control.
A technically correct page is not sufficient if it adds administration or does not help you decide.

## Track jobs and applications

**Jobs** owns the complete opportunity lifecycle. Existing Pipeline links redirect here. Its filters
are views of the same records, not separate copies. Discovery follows the server's shared order and
holds every recommended opportunity, including adverts no longer confirmed open that Best opportunities
leaves out; **Show more** expands it incrementally. Active work can remain active even if a public advert closes.

Open **Open brief** to read **Work assessment**, source links, relevant contacts, application and CV
controls. Use **I applied** only after applying yourself. **More application options** contains
shortlisting and other valid status changes. **Sources and details** holds history and editing.

The job's **Work assessment** starts with availability, pay, engagement, location, remit and fit.
**Can't decide yet because** identifies missing availability or remit, or when both pay and
engagement are unstated. Further recorded gaps appear under **What is still missing for a
decision**. A sourced duty quote takes precedence over the short recorded summary; when the
full summary differs, it remains under **Demand evidence and sources** with supporting evidence. These labels explain
what is known; they do not change your shortlist, application status or recorded commitments.

### Keep the working list manageable

All unchosen recommendations and candidates awaiting checks share **ten places**, including older
manual entries and records you edited. A previous note does not make a record a permanent priority.
A separate pool of up to **thirty candidates** can receive research before winning a visible place.
At most **sixty new records enter automatically in any seven days**; model-call budgets are unchanged.
Explicitly shortlisted jobs and active processes stay separate. You can still save jobs or add
incoming roles manually without an admission limit.

Extra discoveries stay in the source store and are reconsidered during later imports; they do not
create job records or employers merely to fill a backlog. Existing records stay in the folded
**Jobs → History and reference → Reference** section with their history. Unresolved unchosen candidates older than fourteen days leave the working list unless a current source confirms the advert is open;
stronger current evidence can earn them a place again. Repeated sightings alone do not renew them.
You do not need to clear these records one by one.

If the automatic allowance is full, a stronger new discovery waits. You can still use **Save to shortlist**
or **Add incoming role** for work you choose yourself. These limits do not delete old records or set a
fixed size for your preserved history.

### Check vacancy freshness and relevant contacts

The label on each **Jobs** row says whether the advert still looks open, and why. The brief shows a separate check for the suggested contact. Use **Source and context** for that contact’s evidence, or **Sources and details → Vacancy check** for the exact time and source.

An advert looks open while **its source listed it in the daily read** (Adzuna, Reed or the employer's job board) or **a research read confirmed it**, within the last 48 hours. A page that refused or failed to load is not evidence either way. Only those open adverts appear in **Best opportunities**. A card's LinkedIn **received** date identifies the email, not a fresh vacancy check.

| Label | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Still listed on Adzuna · seen …** / **… on the employer's Greenhouse job board · seen …** | The daily read of that source still listed the advert less than 48 hours ago. An aggregator listing is the source's own claim, not the employer's. |
| **Advert open · checked …** | A research read confirmed the advert, or found it on the employer's job board, less than 48 hours ago. |
| **Not listed by … since …** / **Last confirmed open …** | The last listing or confirmation is at least 48 hours old. The advert stays in **Jobs**, not **Best opportunities**. |
| **Open unknown · last check …** / **Open unknown · not checked yet** | There is no current confirmation. The date is the actual source-check attempt; receipt and record-edit dates do not count. |
| **Advert closed · source reports …** / **… closing date … has passed** / **Advert reported closed · checked …** | The source closed it, its closing date has passed, or a read explicitly reported closure. |
| **Contact route confirmed · checked …** / **… not verified** / **… needs recheck** / **… unverified · attempted …** | A contact route check: confirmed less than 24 hours ago, never checked, older than 24 hours, or attempted without success. |

The 48-hour window measures **when the source last listed or confirmed the advert**, not when the role was posted. Age labels update while the page is open and when you return to it; that display update does not reread the source.

**Current contact route** labels each check as a **Personal route** or **Via** their employer; one
never stands in for the other. A public-page check follows the 24-hour rule. A route you confirmed,
or one matched to your own earlier correspondence, keeps its date (**not a fresh public check**,
**current reachability unknown**) and is never relabelled as freshly verified.

**Historical connection**, **Formerly at** and **Former colleague at** preserve useful past relationships. **Recorded role**, **Recorded at** and **Recruiter at** describe saved details and do not by themselves verify present employment. Read the contact check's explanation: checking a historical connection today does not make it a current employer relationship. Neither kind of check proves suitability, hiring authority or an offered introduction.

On a job, **Connections** shows saved connections and **Review profile** where a profile
exists. If no named route is shown, a published general employer contact may appear with its
original review date and **Employer source**. Read its stated scope: a future-opportunities mailbox
does not establish who handles the current vacancy. **Find the hiring manager** opens a LinkedIn
people search using the employer and role. Search results need checking; opening the search does
not identify or verify a hiring manager. Opening a profile or source sends nothing.

### Add a recruiter role or an existing interview

Use **Add incoming role** in **Jobs** when a recruiter approaches you, someone refers
you, or a hiring process starts elsewhere. If the role is already saved, open it and use **Record
existing process** under **Hiring process**.

Enter the title, employer if known, agency separately, how it started and the factual dates. Choose
what actually happened, such as **Recruiter screening completed**. Leave the next step blank if it
has not been agreed. For a booked call, the stage date is the booking date; put the appointment in
the next step. A vacancy URL is optional. Paste the relevant message/specification or upload up to
three PDF, DOCX, TXT or MD files, each at most 1 MiB. Label preparation separately from a specification.
Scanned or unreadable files need pasted text; the form keeps your entries when extraction fails.

Press **Review process**. An exact source match updates the existing role. Similar titles require a
choice between the existing record and a separate process; title similarity does not merge them.
Check the employer, agency and stage, then **Save process**. The same record opens in **Jobs**,
with its original discovery history and notes retained. A shortlist stays in **Jobs → Shortlisted**;
a conversation or interview appears in **Jobs → Active**. No application or CV submission is inferred.

**Hiring process** shows the dated origin, stage, next step and **Private source evidence**. Uploaded
files download from the private workspace. Preparation is proposed wording, not a record of what
was said or verified proof. These files are included in full backups, not slim exports.

Active processes qualify for focused research independently of discovery rank. The record shows
whether it is **Outside scheduled research focus** or included, plus any actual last attempt.
Inclusion does not promise a running check or fresh verification. Public advert closure does not
close a recorded conversation. The same work appears on This week without creating another record.

If a record changed during review, keep your source material, reload and review the new version
before resubmitting. Closed or archived processes require history review rather than silent reopening.
For a mistaken intake stage, use **Undo recorded stage** under **Hiring process** while it is the latest stage change. Source evidence stays in history.
The intake does not monitor recruiter mail: add each new private conversation deliberately.

### Job and contact research

On **Dashboard → Site functions**, open **Job and contact research → Research coverage**. It shows
actual attempts, confirmed checks, unresolved gaps, waiting work, allowance and reuse. A partial
pass is not complete coverage. A page visit or Refresh does not start research.

**Latest research pass** describes that run's checked targets and contact routes, not the whole
network. A recorded zero is a measured result; a missing count stays unknown. Allowance figures
are the snapshot recorded with that pass, not necessarily the balance now. A role's own last
attempt can be older than the Dashboard's latest run; its card and detail keep that attempt's date
and outcome together.

Research runs at **09:00 and 19:00 London time** for active/shortlisted work and ten additional
opportunities. Six of those ten slots are reserved for promising candidates that need
qualification, including supported job alerts and unresolved essential requirements; unused slots are
shared. **Needs checking** shows up to six of those targets and their actual attempt
status. Those jobs remain in **History and reference → Reference** until they qualify, and need open-vacancy evidence for
**Best opportunities**.
Targets rotate by oldest attempt; active work wins ties. Existing evidence is reused when context
allows. Caps remain six model calls per pass, twelve per day and thirty
attempted targets per pass; see [all shared limits](docs/vps-research.md#durable-usage-caps).
A blocked source, unavailable research service and failed check have different explanations. They
do not confirm that an advert is open or make earlier evidence current. Some receipts record only
that research paused, without distinguishing the run cap, daily/weekly or provider allowance, or a busy worker. That
message does not establish a daily limit or a reset time, and the next scheduled pass is not a
promise that this role will be retried. Technical reasons remain in **Research coverage** and
Dashboard failure details for diagnosis.
When four or more completed passes in a row confirm nothing (no advert open or closed, contact
route or fully checked target), the status reads **Nothing confirmed in N passes — needs
attention** and names the last pass that confirmed something.

For a failed, blocked or overdue run, report its displayed reason and time in the work-ops chat.
After repair, look for a newer successful receipt and the evidence you need. **Dashboard** shows
caretaker checks and operational decisions. `/caretaker` asks the coding agent to
investigate within existing authority; it does not itself authorize deployment.

### LinkedIn email alerts

Supported job-alert emails use the existing **15-minute** server collection, without your Mac
being on. LinkedIn alerts and Jack and Jill role digests use the same filters, research queue and
ranking. Current activation evidence distinguishes implemented
support from verified forwarding; Jack and Jill activation is pending.

Eligible roles compete for the existing working/research places and weekly addition allowance.
A parsed email may therefore be retained pending rather than added immediately. Promising,
unverified roles enter **Needs checking** when admitted; verified strong roles compete in
**This week → Best opportunities**, showing their source. Originals stay in Gmail. A repeated role
updates the same record, preserving your decisions and its original age. This covers exact configured
senders, not all LinkedIn posts or recruiter conversations.

| What you need | Where to look |
| --- | --- |
| A role and its next action | **Jobs → Open brief**: remit, fit, evidence, terms, unknowns, CV and application link. |
| Processing and delivery status | **Dashboard → Site functions → Job alert email processing → Email delivery details**: last attempt, last successful check, separate source counts/last processing, failures and import deferrals. |
| The original alert | Gmail → **Work-ops/LinkedIn alerts**, or search `from:jobalerts-noreply@linkedin.com`; Jack and Jill originals use `from:jack@jackandjill.ai`. |

**Working** means a successful processing run and at least one alert processed. **Waiting for first job alert** means delivery has not yet been demonstrated. **Failed**, **Incomplete** or
**Overdue** needs attention; **No recent alerts — check delivery** means there has been no processed
alert for 36 hours and you should compare with Gmail.

If an expected advert is missing, check the original email and the panel, then give the email's

acting. A new email does not renew either check.

when you choose **Compare candidates**. Decision cards open the actual choices and their consequences.

Expand **Are better opportunities being excluded?**, then choose **Compare candidates** to see
up to three candidates excluded by capacity and three excluded by the recommendation rules alongside
the three weakest unchosen jobs receiving research. Rankings
use the same evidence as intake, not a promise of suitability. Each row states its reason and unknowns.
The comparison is dated and covers current unsaved adverts available to automatic intake. Earlier
imported files and adverts rejected by the initial filters are outside it; if its input limit is
reached it explicitly reports partial coverage. An unreadable source store offers **Retry comparison**.
Refresh it after changing your shortlist; saved results can become stale.

Use **Review lead → Shortlist** to include an excluded email role in chosen research, or **Jobs →
Save to shortlist** for another source. Open a maintained job and **Skip** it only if it is unsuitable.
These are separate choices, not an automatic swap. Active and shortlisted processes are protected
from the weakest-maintained comparison. Skipping releases research attention; it does not refund
the rolling automatic-additions allowance. Reading, shortlisting and skipping never submit an application.

The worker prioritises public questions that can settle a work decision, with fewer unresolved public
blockers first; chosen work and discovery keep alternating. Equal priorities rotate by oldest
substantive attempt. A personal experience gap alone does not use a research call. Existing allowance
caps still apply, and one target can require multiple calls. The page does not start a research pass.

The server-only figures (capacity, quotas, 30-day counts and responsiveness) are read when you open
the Dashboard, when you return to it and every five minutes while it is visible; other tabs never

it says so with **Retry**; earlier figures stay, labelled with their time, rather than showing as
current. A part the server cannot read says **Unknown** with the reason rather than showing zero.
A coverage gap or a high meter is information, not a task: report it in a work-ops chat if it
persists. To have a failure investigated, send `/caretaker`.

## Follow progress and results

Work progress is in **Jobs**, with its active subset on **This week**. Personal contact, replies and
current next steps stay in **People**. All use the same canonical records and correction history.
Historical Pipeline links remain usable through redirects. Elapsed time does not turn an unanswered
personal message into a follow-up task or a no-response outcome.

## Use Research when you need it

Research evidence belongs to the work assessment and source details in **Jobs**. Old Research links
redirect there. Company and agency records remain reachable through linked work, people and search.
**People** retains **Import LinkedIn CSV** and **Imported people to review**. Email alert ingestion and
incoming hiring-process evidence are separate from the retired outgoing-message workflow.

## When something goes wrong

| Situation | Recovery |
| --- | --- |
| Save says **Your sign-in has ended** | Keep this tab open. Use **Sign in in a new tab**, sign in, return and retry. Earlier successful saves remain. |
| Save fails or the connection is interrupted | Keep your input. Check the record/history before retrying an uncertain result. Repeated clicks while a request is pending do not create another request. |
| **This record changed** | Preserve your unsaved factual note, refresh and review the latest record before retrying. The stale revision is refused. |
| Wrong event or status | Use **Undo** immediately, or **Sources and details → More → Correct the last entry** / **Correct the last status change**. History retains the correction. |
| A current next step is wrong | For a job use **Update work state → Next action** or its hiring-process controls. For People/direct work use **Update next step**; clearing and completion are explicit. |
| Expected opportunity is absent from Discovery | Check **Shortlisted**, **Active**, **Closed / skipped** and **History and reference → Reference**. A prior skip, duplicate, exclusion or missing evidence can explain it. |
| Research is waiting | Inspect the attempt date and **Research coverage**. Report a failed, blocked or overdue run in the work-ops chat. A generic pause does not identify an allowance-reset time; do not treat old evidence as current. |
| Research says **Nothing confirmed in N passes** | Research keeps running and spending its calls. Ask for a diagnosis in a work-ops chat; there is nothing to triage record by record. |
| A saved draft or old approval appears unfinished | Read it in History. It is preserved historical content, not a task to complete before contacting anyone. |
| Missing contact detail | Use **Add email address** / **Add phone number** or **Sources and details → Edit details**. Do not guess a detail. |
| Missing emailed role | Compare **Dashboard → Job alert email processing** with the Gmail original; report its subject/link and attempt time. |
| Company logo is missing or fails | Initials are the expected fallback. Use the name, source and evidence as usual; a missing image does not indicate a closed vacancy. |
| Vacancy or contact **needs recheck** | Read its source explanation. A record edit or new alert does not refresh the evidence timestamp. |
