# The product idea

[Repository home](../README.md)

The workspace is also the working prototype for a product: a candidate research service that
outplacement providers could offer under their own brand. This is the proposal, as the
interactive pitch linked from the [README](../README.md#about) presents it.

**Your next job is probably one conversation away.**

career/ops helps people find suitable work and a reason to get in touch. For outplacement providers, it is an opportunity to offer candidates research support between coaching sessions, using software that is already built and running.

- **Find suitable work and people who can help.** Bring relevant roles, evidence of companies looking for help and people you know into the same search. See why an opportunity fits your experience and whether a contact could help you understand the work before you apply.

- **Spend less time sorting through job listings.** Get a small list with the research beside it: what the work involves, how it matches your experience and what still needs checking. Spend your time deciding what to pursue and preparing for conversations, with applications already under way kept in view.

- **Support candidates between coaching sessions.** Add a candidate research service to your existing programme, under your own brand. The proposed adviser view brings selected roles and questions into the session, so advisers can spend more time helping candidates choose and prepare. License or acquire the working software to build that service.

**The research is built.** The software already collects opportunities, investigates selected leads
and assesses their fit:

- **Relevant work for each candidate.** The software combines job adverts, selected alerts and company information with the candidate’s experience and preferences. AI research investigates selected opportunities. This gives the candidate specific roles and questions to take into a conversation with an adviser.

- **Reasons behind the recommendations.** Each assessment explains the fit, practical terms and unanswered questions, with sources to check. An adviser can examine the reasoning, challenge a weak match and help the candidate decide where to spend their time.

- **Less software to build.** Collection, research, assessment and application tracking are already implemented in a running prototype. The code comes with tests and operating documentation. A partner’s development can focus on the candidate and adviser service, integrations and rollout.
