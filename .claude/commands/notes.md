Scheduled email preparation and stored draft approvals are retired. People supports temporary
message preparation; sending takes place in the external channel.
Do not generate/store card messages, create draft approvals, alter saved draft decisions, or re-enable
weekly person selection. Existing notes, immutable versions, sent wording and evaluation previews
remain read-only history. $ARGUMENTS

For current use, follow [the user guide](../../USER-GUIDE.md#the-note). If the owner is asking for a
specific new task, establish that task from his request; do not silently revive this command's old
five-person workflow. Preserve saved evidence and use the canonical reader for a requested record.
For previous cover letters, CVs and reusable wording outside the site, follow
[reference-data ownership](../../docs/reference-data.md) to the private local message library.
Read the delivery queue for current direction and deployment status.
