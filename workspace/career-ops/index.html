The technology

The research is built.
Make it part of your service.

The value to a provider is research that a candidate and adviser can use to make a decision. career/ops already collects opportunities, investigates selected leads and assesses their fit. A partner can build its service around that work, with its own brand and approach to coaching.

  • Relevant work for each candidate

    The software combines job adverts, selected alerts and company information with the candidate’s experience and preferences. AI research investigates selected opportunities. This gives the candidate specific roles and questions to take into a conversation with an adviser.

  • Reasons behind the recommendations

    Each assessment explains the fit, practical terms and unanswered questions, with sources to check. An adviser can examine the reasoning, challenge a weak match and help the candidate decide where to spend their time.

  • Less software to build

    Collection, research, assessment and application tracking are already implemented in a running prototype. The code comes with tests and operating documentation. A partner’s development can focus on the candidate and adviser service, integrations and rollout.

For a provider, the opportunity is to offer continuing job-search support alongside its advisers’ expertise. Licensing provides a basis for a branded service; acquisition gives the buyer ownership of the software and its future development.

In everyday use

Help candidates keep going
between sessions.

Candidates need roles to consider and questions to pursue between appointments. The software keeps researching selected opportunities and retains their applications and notes, so they can return to the search without starting again.

How opportunities are selected

The current prototype limits how much new work it puts in front of the user:

  1. Collect the sourcesEmployer adverts, selected job alerts and public requests for work are imported into the same records.
  2. Limit the listThe list holds up to ten opportunities, including those still being checked. At most ten are added automatically in seven days.
  3. Keep applications separateShortlisted opportunities and active applications stay separate, with their recorded progress and next step.
  4. Retain earlier researchEarlier records remain searchable without appearing on the list for review.
How information is checked

Adverts can close and source pages can become unavailable. The software records what its checks establish:

  1. Repeat source checksSource reads and focused research revisit opportunities. An unavailable page or incomplete check stays visible as a gap.
  2. Show sources and missing detailsThe assessment explains the work, relevant experience, practical terms and what remains unknown. Source dates help you judge how current the evidence is.
  3. Preserve the user’s recordsFresh evidence can change what deserves attention. Your edits, recorded choices and history stay intact, and you decide whether to change direction.

A completed research pass does not mean every fact is confirmed. You can see what was checked and what still needs attention.

What advisers would be able to see

The proposed adviser account would show the opportunities and progress a candidate chooses to share before a session.

  • For candidatesA private workspace, with control over the opportunities, context and progress they choose to share.
  • For advisersThe roles a candidate is considering, why they were recommended and the questions still to resolve.
  • For providersYour own branded service, with reporting agreed for the pilot and access controlled by the candidate.

Adviser access, candidate sharing controls and programme reporting belong to the next commercial build.
